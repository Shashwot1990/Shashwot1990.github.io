About Me

Hello! I'm Shashwot Niraula, a Geospatial Application Developer & Analyst at WSP USA. I build, deploy, and maintain GIS software across the Esri ArcGIS ecosystem — Experience Builder, ArcGIS Online/Enterprise, Survey123, ArcGIS Dashboards, and ArcPy automation — working directly with state and municipal government clients.

Right now I own the expansion of a cloud-based geospatial asset inventory platform across the Esri suite: designing new features, consolidating data across many asset classes, and establishing the application architecture and standards my teammates build against. I pair a Computer Science degree with academic certificates in GIS and AI/ML to deliver full-stack geospatial applications, relational-database-backed data pipelines, and AI-powered mapping tools.

Beyond work, I'm an avid explorer who loves hiking in pristine landscapes, playing soccer and tennis, and occasionally indulging in cooking. My multicultural background lets me speak English, Nepali, and Hindi fluently.
