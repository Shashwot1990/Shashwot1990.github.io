Gratitude Day

कृतज्ञता दिन

तिम्रो मायाको लागि, तिम्रो स्याहारको लागि धन्यवाद। तिमी मेरो जीवनको सबैभन्दा ठूलो उपहार हौ।

Thank you for loving me when I'm difficult, for understanding me when I'm confused. Thank you for being YOU. You are my greatest blessing.

Forever yours 💕
