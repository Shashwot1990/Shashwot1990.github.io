Chocolate Day

चकलेटको दिन

तिमीसँगको जीवन चकलेट जस्तै मिठो छ। हरेक दिन तिमीसँग बिताउनु एउटा नयाँ स्वाद चाख्नु जस्तै हो।

Life with you is sweeter than the finest chocolate. Each moment we share is like tasting a new flavor of happiness. You are the sweetness in my life.

Forever yours 💕
